For all the perfectionists out there: you do not need to know everything before you can start doing something.

counterintuitively, i would say (tbh, it's not mine, i'm steal it :3) that by doing it you will know and understand it (by the time)

When we learn a lot but rarely apply it, we form an illusion of knowing. Learning itself doesn't guarantee understanding. We may become fluent with the material, be able to recognize terms, repeat explanations, or follow a lecture - but fluency is not the same as mastery.

we are often poor judges of whether we truly know something. that is why we need concrete evidence. One of the best forms of evidence is the thing we can make after learning something, or the problem we can solve with it.

Also, the learning part was not that hard, we *thought* it hard and believe by doing it, we have make some progress. In reality, when we do something, we put ourself in a test, exposes the possibility that we do not understand what we have invested so much effort into. *That is the hard part*.

Our unconscious mind knows this. So it tries to protect us by insisting that we prepare perfectly before we begin. That is one reason perfectionism appears, we avoid acting until we feel we know everything.

But often, the real fear is failure. We are afraid to fail, afraid to discover that we have failed, and afraid to confront the possibility that we do not know as much as we thought.

Before, we may have been afraid without realizing it. Now, we can recognize that fear and make a choice: face it or not. Either way, at least we can see what is happening.
